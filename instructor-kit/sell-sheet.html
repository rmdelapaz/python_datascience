Why Instructors Choose It

  • One dataset, start to finish. Pinecrest Outfitters, a fictional outdoor retailer: two years of orders, store traffic, weather, a survey, a checkout experiment, a SQLite database and a messy export with 22 documented flaws. No privacy risk.
  • Web lessons that never hit a real site. A practice shop to scrape (online, or served from a zip) and a practice API on each laptop, with keys, tokens and rate limits. Saved fixtures let every lab run offline.
  • Honest pacing. Each 75–110-minute lesson is split: mental model, live demo, guided lab and project start in the room; the rest at home, about two to three hours a week.
  • Only free tools. Python 3.12+, JupyterLab or VS Code, Chrome, open-source libraries. No accounts, no paid keys.
  • For trainers, bootcamps, colleges and CE, corporate L&D and workforce programs; weekly, twice weekly or intensive.
